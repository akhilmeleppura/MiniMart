## 🌐 Live Demo & Application Access

Explore **MiniMart**, a multi-vendor eCommerce management platform built with Laravel.

**Live Application:** [https://minimart-hqui.onrender.com/login](https://minimart-hqui.onrender.com/login)

**GitHub Repository:** [https://github.com/akhilmeleppura/MiniMart](https://github.com/akhilmeleppura/MiniMart)

### 🔑 Demo Accounts

The live application includes two preconfigured showcase accounts with different access levels.

| Role | Email | Password | Access Level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Supreme Admin | `admin@gmail.com` | `Admin@123` | Full Access |
| Store Manager | `manager@gmail.com` | `Manager@123` | Medium Role |

**How to Access**
1. Open the [MiniMart Live Application](https://minimart-hqui.onrender.com/login).
2. Select a showcase role to automatically fill in its login credentials, or enter them manually.
3. Click the login button to explore the dashboard and available features.

> **Demo Environment:** These accounts are provided exclusively for application evaluation and demonstration. Access to features depends on the assigned role and permissions.
